# Palm Fever Cover Studio

Palm Fever is an offline, GTA-style multi-panel cover studio that turns a photo and a caption into Vice City-style cover art. Drop in your photos, type a caption and a sub-line, and it renders a canvas mosaic of eight photo panels — each graded to an ink-comic look with heavy contrast, duotone sunset tints, halftone dots, and a film-grain vignette — crowned with bold gradient lettering outlined in black and white. Choose a colour grade (Sunset, Neon Night, or Golden), fill each panel by clicking it and fine-tune with drag and scroll, then export the finished cover as a PNG in one of three sizes: 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs locally in the browser with plain static files and ES modules — no libraries, no network requests, and your photos never leave your machine.
